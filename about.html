VIT Vellore

Vellore Institute of Technology, Tamil Nadu

VIT Vellore is a leading technology university whose mission is to give students world-class education, research opportunities and industry exposure. The School of Computer Science and Engineering (SCOPE) offers integrated and postgraduate programmes. Students build real projects, join clubs and intern with top recruiters. The campus has labs, libraries, a health centre and sports facilities.
